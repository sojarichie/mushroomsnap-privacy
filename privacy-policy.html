MushroomSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 8, 2026

Information We Collect

MushroomSnap collects the following information: email address for account creation, photos you submit for mushroom identification, and scan history stored in your account.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the mushroom identification service, manage your account, and store your scan history. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

Photos

Photos submitted for identification are processed by an AI service and are not permanently stored on our servers.

Subscriptions

Subscription payments are processed by Apple or Google. We do not store payment information.
